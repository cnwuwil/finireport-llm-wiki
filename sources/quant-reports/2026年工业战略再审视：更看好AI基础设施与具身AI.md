# China Industrial Outlook 2026

# Initial signs of improving trends

During 1Q26, we see better trends in relation to China Industrial sector including:

1) FAI – first time for sub-sector property, infra, manufacturing all positive during   
2M26 vs 2025,

2) the possible start of China MFG inventory restocking,

3) robust China export growth,

4) improving Japan machine orders to China with stronger growth rates in recent months.

# 1) Improving FAI trend

The sub-FAI of property, infra and manufacturing all turned positive as $0 \%$ , 11. $4 \%$ and $3 . 1 \%$ respectively during 2M26. Property FAI in early 2026 was better than persistent decline range of $9 . 1 \%$ to $1 7 . 5 \%$ in 2025. Infra FAI in early 2026 was stronger than 2H25’s range from $- 1 . 5 \%$ to $+ 7 . 3 \%$ . Mfg FAI in early 2026 also showed faster growth than $+ 0 . 6 \%$ to $2 . 7 \%$ during 4Q25. The muted economic growth in 2H25 may cause China govt to underpin positive growth of Infra and Mfg (particularly for advanced manufacturing) to counter the risk of an economic slowdown.

# 3) China export maintains strong growth momentum

China export growth was robust in 2025 and even surged to almost $40 \%$ in Feb 2026. See Fig 10. We attribute it to China’s success on:

1) Market diversification into ASEAN ( $1 3 \%$ in 2025 and accelerating to $\sim 3 0 \%$ in early 2026), Africa ${ \sim } 2 6 \%$ in 2025 and further rose to $34 \%$ in 2M26), EU ${ \sim } 8 \%$ in 2025 but grew ${ \sim } 2 8 \%$ in 2M26) though China exports to N America down $20 \%$ in 2025 but shrinking to a slump of ${ \sim } 1 1 \%$ in early 2026.

2) High-tech and green export like NEV ( $5 5 \%$ in 2025 and even rose to ${ \sim } 6 7 \%$ during 2M26), Li-ion batteries $( \sim 3 8 \%$ in 2025, further stronger to ${ > } 4 0 \%$ in early 2026), solar cell $( > 7 0 \%$ in 2025, a modest decline during 2M26 for high base), 3D printing equipment $( \sim 4 5 \%$ in 2025, further to $\sim 5 4 \%$ in early 2026), semi $14 \%$ in 2025, but soared by $570 \%$ in 2M26), industrial robot ( $2 8 \%$ in 2025, further rose to $5 3 0 \%$ in early 2026).

3) Muted domestic demand causing lower export price – the soft domestic demand has been dragging prices which makes China exports even more competitive.

# 4) Japan machine orders to China imply acceleration in early 2026

Per JMTBA, Japan machine orders to China acts as a leading indicator for China advanced manufacturing. The yoy growth rate was slower at single digit of $2 \% - 8 \%$ during Jun-Aug 2025. The growth momentum then accelerated starting from Sep at $2 5 \%$ , Oct at $10 \%$ , Nov at $30 \%$ , and Dec at $2 \%$ . However, the growth rates further 研报添加accelerated to $57 \%$ LCD1233 in Jan 2026 and $40 \%$ ad 04-16 in Feb 2026.

The strong annual growth suggests Chinese firms are aggressively upgrading to higher-end Japanese machinery. Key drivers are 1) China equipment renewal policy, and 2) automation and robotics driven as demand is shifting away from simply assembly towards complex 5-axis machining and high-precision tools used in EV components and semiconductor equipment. This leading indicator would suggest a production ramp-up in the electronics / auto sectors for 3Q26.

This robust Japan machine order seems in contrast with slower momentum of China NBS MFG PMI below 50 during 2M26 (i.e., 49.3 in Jan and 49.0 in Feb). This reflects large SOE (state-owned enterprises) are still facing soft domestic demand. This is in line with quite flattish China Business Climate Index as Mar 2025 quarter at 108.5, Jun quarter at 108.5, Sep quarter at 108.9 and Dec quarter at 108.4.

In contrast, China Caixin PMI datapoint had a recent uptick from latest trough of 49.9 in Nov 2025 to subsequently improved 50.1 in Dec, 50.3 in Jan and 52.1 in Feb. China Caixin reflects SME and export manufacturing which are increasingly 研报添加微信 LCD123321Cad 04-16 expanding manufacturing activities on automation, robots and external demand.
