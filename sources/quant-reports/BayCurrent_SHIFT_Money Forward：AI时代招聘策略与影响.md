# Japan Technology: IT Services: BayCurrent/SHIFT/Money Forward read-across: Hiring strategies for the AI era

20 April 2026 | 3:46PM JST

Within this note we summarize the takeaways from our recent meetings with three Not Covered companies and the read-across for our coverage—BayCurrent Consulting (strategy/IT consulting), SHIFT (software testing), and Money Forward 订阅研报添加微信 LCD123321Cad 04-(cloud accounting/HR software for mid-sized and small businesses). See our recent reports for more details on each company’s Nov 2025-Feb 2026 results: BayCurrent/SHIFT/Money Forward.

The three companies are regarded within the equity market as related to AI disruption, and their share price performance was relatively muted within the sector prior to their results announcements. We highlight market concerns around the impact of AI disruption on software testing (SHIFT’s main business) and SaaS (Money Forward). SHIFT has changed its previous mass hiring strategy to one of curbing hiring, while Money Forward is already curtailing its hiring via operational efficiency gains from AI utilization, with a positive impact on earnings starting to materialize. For BayCurrent, which specializes in consulting, the penetration of AI has created new demand for AI implementation support consulting, and the company is stepping up hiring to meet this growing demand.

Each company is shifting to what it considers to be its optimal hiring strategy at present—albeit with differences among the three—driven by the penetration of generative AI. In the IT services industry, where the shortage of engineers is becoming more acute, we are focusing on companies that are shifting to hiring and M&A strategies that emphasize efficiency and productivity improvements. From this perspective, we have a positive view of companies like Fujitsu (Buy), which has abolished its practice of mass new graduate hiring and is focusing on generative AI-driven development. We will be watching for commentary from sector companies in their upcoming Jan-Mar earnings announcements.

BayCurrent Consulting (6532.T) (see report for recent results) Three points of focus: hiring trends, AI projects, and demand trends from major 订阅研报添加微信 LCD123321Cad clients. First, while consultant hiring is being curbed globally due primarily to the penetration of generative AI, competition for consultant hiring in Japan remains intense. This is likely due to Japan-specific factors, such as on-site labor shortages caused by a declining working population, in addition to the emergence of new consulting needs related to AI implementation. BayCurrent is further strengthening its hiring and plans to hire 710 new graduates (vs. 512 in FY2/26) and 1,560 mid-career professionals (vs. 1,386) in FY2/27 (includes non-consultants). The

company is expanding its regional satellite offices and focusing on secure top talent in regional Japan.

Second, consulting projects related to generative AI are on an upward trend. According to the company, about $5 0 \mathrm { - } 6 0 \%$ of all its projects are AI-related, with strong demand primarily for data preparation for utilizing AI, business process re-engineering (BPR) using AI, launching AI-related organizations, and strategies for hiring AI talent. AI adoption is still in its initial phase, and we expect it to serve as a new driver of market expansion for the next several years.

# SHIFT (3697.T) (see report for recent results)

Two points of focus: hiring policy and pricing model. First, SHIFT is revising its previous mass hiring strategy and will switch to a hiring policy focused on improving productivity, concentrating future hiring in high-value-added fields such as AI engineers. The background for this change in hiring policy is the growing reality that generative AI 订阅研报添加微信 LCD123321Cad 04-21 could replace some demand for software testing in the future. To prepare for the risk of AI disruption, the company plans to: (1) emphasize AI skills for both new graduates and mid-career hires and expand its AI business with a focus on AI modernization, and (2) as a general rule, stop hiring for personnel in areas like the back office, with plans to shift some of these personnel to profit centers such as Business Process as a Service (BPaaS). In FY8/26, it plans to curb hiring by 440 people compared to its internal plan, mainly in profit centers, and the company expects the full-year hiring number to be near the lower end of its guidance range of 2,500-2,700 people. On a quarterly basis, the number of hires in 1Q was 678. According to the company, 2Q and 3Q will be at a similar level, with a plan to decrease the number of hires significantly from 4Q. The company stated that it will continue a similar policy in FY8/27 and anticipates a gradual increase in the total number of employees on a net basis.

Second, the company is gradually changing its pricing structure away from a business model based on person-month pricing. Specifically, for project pricing, it is shifting toward proposing fixed prices based on the total volume of testing. However, looking at the industry as a whole, we believe the penetration of generative AI could intensify downward pressure on software testing prices. The company shares this view and has a policy to lower project prices to capture a larger volume of work.

# Money Forward (3994.T) (see report for recent results)

One point of focus: customer acquisition efficiency and AI utilization. The CAC payback period (the period required to recover the cost of customer acquisition with profits) for the corporate business within the Business segment in 1Q11/26 (Dec-Feb) was 14 months, continuing its trend of improvement both yoy and qoq (vs. 18-19

months in the same period of the previous year and 15 months in the previous quarter). The improvement is mainly driven by business for SMEs, where sales via accounting firms and price hikes have been successful. While many SaaS companies have a direct sales structure and tend to hire large numbers of sales personnel to expand sales, Money Forward is intentionally curbing hiring with a focus on efficiency through AI utilization, and the growth rate of ARR per employee is on an accelerating trend $( + 1 2 \%$ yoy in 1Q). The company believes it can reduce hiring by about 100 people compared to its initial 订阅研报添加微信 LCD123321Cad 04-21 plan and thinks that efficiency improvements through AI utilization will continue going forward.
